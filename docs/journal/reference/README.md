# Reference links

Links only. Do not download other people's videos or papers into this repo (copyright). Each line says what to learn from it. All URLs were opened and returned HTTP 200 on 2026-10-22.

## Motion: real spiders (video)

- [Tarantula walking, close up (YouTube, 14 s)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgYhQ2mYVqU): which legs move together and how the body rides over them.
- [Chilean Rose Tarantula Walking (YouTube, 33 s)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nzm6-2arnw8): a steady walk at normal speed, good for counting the leg order.
- [World's biggest jumping spider in slow motion (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FhpL5h8sEHo): slow motion shows leg lift and body height that real-time footage hides.

## Motion: real robots (video)

- [How to make Quadruped Spider Robot, Arduino and 3D parts (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDuzb-I00-4): a hobby 12-servo spider-style quadruped, from build to first steps.
- [HariFun #149, 3D-Printed Arduino Spider Robot (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Pos9pE8xwU): a small spider walker and its gait, to compare with our robot.
- [Stanford Pupper (YouTube, 1 min)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIjodHA78UE): a well-known open 12-servo quadruped and how stable its walk looks.

## Motion: papers

- [Design and implementation of a control structure for a quadruped robot (Univ. of Twente)](https://essay.utwente.nl/essays/73116): how a small quadruped's gait controller is layered.
- [Walking Robot Bio-inspired by Insect's Locomotion (SCITEPRESS 2018)](https://www.scitepress.org/Papers/2018/69128/): a quadruped spider robot modelled on a real spider's movement, with kinematics and simulated walking.
- [Design, Simulation, and Testing of a Flexible Actuated Spine for Quadruped Robots (arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.06527): how a spine helps lift a foot, and how simulation was calibrated against hardware.
- [Biomechanics of locomotion in Antarctic sea spiders (SICB)](https://sicb.org/?p=16024): how a many-legged animal sequences its legs, as a contrast to a textbook gait.

## Storytelling

- [Hack Club Stardance: devlog guide](https://stardance.hackclub.com/guides/devlogs): lead with what is new, show visuals, be honest about setbacks.
- [Wayline: ultimate guide to devlogs](https://www.wayline.io/blog/ultimate-guide-devlogs-indie-developers): organize entries around milestones instead of daily task lists.
- [Wayline: fixing a too-detailed dev log](https://www.wayline.io/blog/fixing-too-detailed-dev-log): cut details that only matter inside your own coding session.
- [Bugnet: how to write a devlog](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-write-a-devlog): a simple structure of goals, progress, challenges, solutions, next steps.
